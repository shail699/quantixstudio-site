About

One person. Full ownership, end to end.

Quantix Studio is a one-person independent software studio founded in 2026 by Sohail Ahmed in Pakistan — design, engineering, and support, all from the same desk.

The studio is bootstrapped: no investors, no outside funding. That keeps every decision focused on one thing — software that serves its users well.

My working belief is simple: the best business software is boring in the best way. It works offline, it never loses your data, and it stays out of your way.
